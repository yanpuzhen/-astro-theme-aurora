Aurora 3.0 is a **static-first** theme: content is rendered at build time, while focused Vue islands add search, lightbox, code copy, theme persistence, and mobile navigation.

The result is a calm reading experience with a little colour around the edges. Browse the [archive](/archives/), try the [search](/search/), or switch to the [Chinese welcome post](/post/welcome-to-aurora-3-cn/).
